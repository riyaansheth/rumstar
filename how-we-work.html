How we work

A clear process, from first call to signed contract.

You stay the decision maker throughout. We do the reading, the comparing and the chasing, and we put everything in writing.

Five steps, in order.

Most engagements follow this path. Some stop after step three, when the written recommendation is all a business needs.

Start with step one
  1. 1

    First call

    Fifteen minutes, free. We ask how you take payments today, who your providers are and what is bothering you. By the end you know whether a review is worth doing.

  2. 2

    Statement Review

    You send your recent statements and contracts. We break down every charge, check your pricing model and benchmark it. We confirm the turnaround as soon as we have your documents.

  3. 3

    Written recommendation

    A plain report: what you pay, what you should pay, and how to get there. Renegotiate, move, or change how payments are routed. Options are ranked and every trade-off is explained.

  4. 4

    Delivery

    We run the negotiation, the tender or the migration with the providers. You approve every decision and sign contracts directly with the provider. We never sit between you and your money.

  5. 5

    Keep watching

    Regular statement checks and renewal date tracking, so rates do not creep back up and contracts do not roll over unnoticed.

What we need from you.

The more complete this is, the faster and sharper the review. Send what you have. We will ask for anything missing.

For a Statement Review

  • Your last three months of processing statements, from every provider
  • Your current contracts and pricing schedules
  • Chargeback and fraud reports, if you have them
  • Your website and a description of your checkout
  • Half an hour with whoever looks after payments

For a new account application

  • Company registration documents
  • Identity documents for directors and main shareholders
  • Proof of business address
  • Processing history, where you have it
  • Any licences your sector requires
  • Your website, with terms, refund policy and contact details visible

Sharing documents safely. We agree a secure way to transfer statements and contracts before you send them, and a redacted summary is often enough to start. Never send card numbers, CVVs, passwords or bank login details, to us or anyone else.

Ground rules.

How we behave on every engagement, whatever its size.

  • You decide.We recommend. You approve every change and sign every contract yourself.
  • Disclosed, always.Any commercial relationship we have with a provider is disclosed before you decide anything.
  • In writing.Findings, recommendations and offers are written down, so you can check them and keep them.
  • Confidential.Your statements and contracts stay private. We sign a confidentiality agreement on request.
  • Never in the money flow.We do not process payments or hold funds. Your money moves only between you, your customers and your licensed providers.
  • Honest about the gap.If there is not enough to gain, we tell you after the first call, including when that means not hiring us.
