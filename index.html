Before you call.

The questions people ask first.

Do you take commission from providers?

No. We are paid by you and only you. If a provider offered us a referral fee we would turn it down, and tell you they offered.

How long does a fee review take?

Usually five to ten working days from receiving your statements. Setups with several providers or currencies take a little longer.

What do you need from us?

Three months of processing statements, your current contract, and half an hour with whoever looks after payments.

What does it cost?

The first call is free. After that we quote a fixed fee for the review and a separate fixed fee for any negotiation or migration work. No surprises.

Will we have to switch providers?

Often not. Much of our work is renegotiating with the provider you already have. We only recommend moving when the numbers clearly justify it.
