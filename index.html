Where your rate actually goes.

Your provider shows you one blended rate. Underneath it are five different charges. Two are set by the card schemes. The other three are a negotiation nobody had on your behalf.

5
separate charges hidden inside one blended rate
How each charge works
  • Interchange[x.xx%]Paid to the card issuer. Capped in the EEA for consumer cards.
  • Scheme fees[x.xx%]Set by Visa and Mastercard. Passed through.
  • Acquirer margin[x.xx%]Your provider's cut. Negotiable.
  • Gateway[x.xx%]Often bundled, often overpriced.
  • Extras[x.xx%]PCI, authorisation, refund and statement fees.
