About this process

This is a voluntary process for complaints about Rumstar's consultancy service. It is not a regulated payment-provider complaints procedure.

How to complain

Email support@rumstar.eu or write to RUMSTAR LIMITED, 84 Larnakos, Pallouriotissa, 1046 Nicosia, Cyprus. Include your contact details, engagement reference, what happened and the outcome sought. Do not include card details, passwords or unnecessary customer data.

What happens and when

We aim to acknowledge complaints within five working days and provide a substantive response within twenty working days. If more time is needed, we explain why and give a revised date. These targets do not restrict legal rights or deadlines.

Review and escalation

We review relevant records and explain findings and any proposed remedy. If you disagree, request further review and explain why. Where practicable, someone not directly involved will review it.

Other routes

Privacy complaints may also go directly to the relevant authority, such as the Cyprus Commissioner for Personal Data Protection. Issues with a payment provider's processing or settlement should also be raised with that provider; this process does not replace its statutory procedures. You need not delay seeking legal advice or exercising rights.
