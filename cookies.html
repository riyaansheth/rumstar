In short

rumstar.eu does not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies or third party cookies. It stores two small preferences on your device, and counts visits with analytics that do not use cookies.

What we store on your device

NameTypePurposeKept until
themeLocal storageRemembers whether you chose light or dark mode.You clear your browser data
cookie-noticeLocal storageRemembers that you have seen the cookie notice, so it is not shown again.You clear your browser data

Both are set only by your own actions, are never sent to us and are used only to make the site work the way you asked.

Analytics without cookies

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It does not set cookies, does not use local storage and does not track you across websites.

Fonts and third parties

Our fonts are served from our own server, so loading a page does not send your details to a font provider.

Your choices

You can clear stored preferences at any time through your browser settings. The site works fully without them. You can see the cookie notice again with the link in the footer.

Questions

Email support@rumstar.eu. For how we handle personal data generally, see our Privacy Policy.
