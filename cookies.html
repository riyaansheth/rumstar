Consent and controls

Any legally exempt storage is used only for the relevant transmission or strictly necessary requested-service purpose. Consent-dependent tracking must not run before agreement.

If nonessential tracking is introduced, users must be able to reject it, select purposes and withdraw consent as easily as they give it, while retaining basic content access. Continuing to browse is not consent.

Your browser's site-data controls can clear cookies and localStorage. Clearing theme storage removes the saved preference. Blocking storage can prevent preferences being remembered. These controls supplement, not replace, required consent controls. You can show the cookie notice again with the link in the footer. We update this notice when the inventory changes and gate any new consent-dependent technology before collection.
