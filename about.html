About

Independent advice on how you get paid.

Rumstar is a payment consultancy based in Nicosia, Cyprus. We help businesses pay less to take payments, lose fewer of them, and understand the rules that apply.

Book a free 15 minute call Or call +357 97 692104

Why we exist

Payment providers know their pricing far better than the businesses they charge. Statements are long, fees are split across dozens of lines, and contracts renew quietly. Most businesses never have anyone on their side read them.

That is our job. We read the statements, compare the market, negotiate the terms and oversee the work, then keep checking so the gains last.

How we stay on your side

We recommend what suits your business. Any commercial relationship we have with a provider is disclosed to you before you decide anything. [Rumstar to confirm its fee model and any provider relationships]

What we are not

  • We are not a payment institution or e-money institution.
  • We do not process payments or hold client money.
  • We do not approve or open accounts. Licensed providers do.
  • We are not a law firm. Where a question needs legal advice, we say so.

You can check any payment provider against the public registers kept by the Central Bank of Cyprus.

What we believe.

Four principles that shape every engagement.

  • Numbers before opinions.Every recommendation starts from your statements, not from a sales pitch.
  • Disclose everything.If we have a relationship with a provider, you hear about it first.
  • Write it down.Findings and advice are in writing, so you can hold us to them.
  • You sign, we read first.Contracts are yours. We make sure you know what is in them.
