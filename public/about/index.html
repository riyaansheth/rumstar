About

An independent voice on how you get paid.

Rumstar is a payment consultancy based in Nicosia, Cyprus, founded in 2026 to help businesses pay less to take payments, lose fewer of them and understand the rules that apply.

Why Rumstar exists

Payment providers know their pricing far better than the businesses they charge. Statements are long, fees are spread across dozens of lines, and contracts renew quietly. Most businesses never have anyone on their side read them.

Rumstar was set up to do that job: to read the statements, compare the market, negotiate the terms and oversee the work, then keep checking so the gains last.

How we stay on your side

We recommend what suits your business. Any commercial relationship we have with a provider is disclosed to you before you decide anything. [Rumstar to confirm its fee model and any provider relationships]

What we are not

  • We are not a payment institution or e-money institution.
  • We do not process payments or hold client money.
  • We do not approve or open accounts. Licensed providers do.
  • We are not a law firm. Where a question needs legal advice, we say so.

You can check any payment provider against the public registers kept by the Central Bank of Cyprus.
