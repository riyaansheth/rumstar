01The payment problem

Payments are complicated. Your decisions shouldn’t be.

A card payment passes through several parties, and each takes a share. Your statement compresses all of that into one rate and a monthly total, spread over pages of codes few people read.

Some of those charges are set by the card schemes and capped by law. Others are commercial terms agreed once and rarely revisited. Knowing which is which is the difference between accepting your costs and managing them.

03Independence

Advice that starts with your interests.

Payment providers employ people whose job is to price their services. Most businesses have no one doing the same job in the other direction. That is the role of an independent payment consultant.

  • We act for the business. We do not sell processing, terminals or software, and we do not approve accounts.
  • Relationships are disclosed. Any commercial relationship we have with a provider is disclosed before you decide anything. [Rumstar to confirm its fee model and any provider relationships]
  • Contracts stay yours. You sign directly with licensed providers. We never sit between you and your money.

05Industries

Every model loses money in its own places.

A subscription business, a travel company and a trading platform face different payment problems. We start from how you sell.

  1. 01 Ecommerce and retail Blended pricing that hides what each card costs, cross-border fees on foreign customers, and good payments declined by cautious fraud rules.
  2. 02 Subscriptions and SaaS Renewals failing on expired or replaced cards, customers lost to failed payments rather than cancellations, and authentication friction on recurring charges.
  3. 03 Travel and hospitality Long gaps between payment and delivery, rolling reserves that tie up cash, and chargebacks when plans change.
  4. 04 iGaming and betting Few providers willing to accept the sector, licence checks country by country, and players who expect fast, reliable payouts.
  5. 05 Forex, CFD and trading Card deposits treated as high risk, withdrawals that must return the right way, and regulatory expectations on where client money sits.
  6. 06 Marketplaces and platforms Collecting on behalf of sellers and paying them out, checking sellers before they can be paid, and open questions about whether the model needs a licence.
  7. 07 Digital goods and gaming Instant delivery, so fraud arrives fast; high dispute rates on low value items; and customers in many countries with different payment habits.
  8. 08 Other specialist sectors Nutraceuticals, dating, ticketing and similar sectors face accounts refused or closed with little explanation, and reserves and terms that are hard to compare.
