About payments

What is interchange?

The fee your acquirer pays to the bank that issued your customer's card. In the EEA it is capped for most consumer cards. Read more.

What is the difference between blended and itemised pricing?

Blended pricing is one rate for every card. Itemised pricing passes interchange and scheme fees through at cost and adds a visible margin. Read more.

Can I ask my provider for itemised statements?

In the EEA, yes. The Interchange Fee Regulation requires acquirers to itemise charges by card category and brand unless you ask in writing for blended pricing.

Can I add a surcharge for card payments?

Not for consumer cards covered by the EEA caps. Where surcharging is allowed, it cannot exceed your direct cost. Read more.

Why are good payments declined?

Expired cards, temporary issuer decisions, authentication problems and fraud rules that are too strict. Read more.

What is a rolling reserve?

A share of your takings held back by a provider for a period, to cover future chargebacks. Common in higher risk sectors, and negotiable.
