Our Approach

From first conversation to signed contract.

You remain the decision maker throughout. We do the reading, comparing and chasing, and we put everything in writing.

The five steps

  1. 01

    Discover

    A first conversation about how you take payments today, who your providers are and what is not working.

    You receiveA clear view of whether a review is worthwhile. The first conversation is free and takes about fifteen minutes.

  2. 02

    Analyse

    We read your statements and contracts, separate fixed costs from negotiable ones and look at approvals, disputes and terms.

    You receiveA written analysis, delivered within [X] working days of receiving your documents.

  3. 03

    Recommend

    We set out the options: renegotiate, change provider, adjust routing or authentication, or leave things as they are.

    You receiveA ranked recommendation with the trade-offs explained, in writing.

  4. 04

    Implement

    With your approval, we run the negotiation, tender or migration with the providers involved.

    You receiveAgreed terms and a working set-up. You sign directly with the provider.

  5. 05

    Review

    Rates drift and providers add fees. We keep reading your statements and tracking renewal dates.

    You receiveA short written update at each review, and early warning of anything that has changed.

AMethod

How we work, on every engagement.

Numbers before opinions
Every recommendation starts from your statements and contracts, not from a provider's pitch.
Disclosed, always
Any commercial relationship we have with a provider is disclosed before you decide anything.
In writing
Findings, recommendations and offers are written down, so you can check them and keep them.
Confidential
Your documents are used only for your engagement. We sign a confidentiality agreement on request.
Never in the money flow
We do not process payments or hold funds. Money moves only between you, your customers and licensed providers.
Honest about the gap
If there is not enough to gain, we say so after the first conversation, including when that means not hiring us.

BWhat we need

For a cost or performance review

  • Your last three months of processing statements, from every provider
  • Your current contracts and pricing schedules
  • Chargeback, fraud and decline reports, if available
  • Your website and a description of your checkout
  • Time with whoever looks after payments

For a new account application

  • Company registration documents
  • Identity documents for directors and main shareholders
  • Proof of business address
  • Processing history, where you have it
  • Any licences your sector requires
  • Your website, with terms, refund policy and contact details visible

CPractical examples

What an engagement can look like.

Illustrative scenarios showing how the process applies. They are not client cases.

A subscription business losing renewals

Discover. Renewals fail more often than expected, and customers are lost without cancelling.

Analyse. Decline reasons, card updater coverage, retry timing and the exemptions requested on renewals.

Recommend. Network tokens, retry logic within scheme rules, and correct flagging of merchant initiated payments.

An online retailer on an old blended rate

Discover. One rate agreed years ago, never reviewed, with a contract that renews automatically.

Analyse. Card mix, pass-through costs, margin and the full fee schedule against the market.

Recommend. Itemised pricing, a renegotiation with the current provider and a tender as the fallback.

A trading platform needing a new provider

Discover. A provider has given notice, and the sector limits the options.

Analyse. Licences, processing history, website compliance and the controls providers will ask about.

Recommend. A complete application file and a shortlist of providers with appetite for the sector.
