Payment Cost Optimisation

A line by line reading of what you pay to accept payments, what you should be paying, and how to close the gap.

  • Statement Review
  • Pricing model review
  • Contract renegotiation on price
  • Ongoing statement monitoring

AThe problem

Most businesses see one blended rate and a monthly total. Underneath sit interchange, scheme fees, the acquirer's margin, gateway charges and a long tail of smaller fees. Some are fixed by the card schemes. Others are commercial terms that were never seriously negotiated.

Pricing also drifts. Scheme fees change, new line items appear, introductory rates expire and contracts renew automatically. Without someone reading the statements regularly, costs rise without anyone deciding they should.

BWhat we evaluate

Your statements
Every charge, separated into pass-through costs and provider margin.
Your pricing model
Whether you are on blended or itemised pricing, and which suits your card mix.
Your card mix
Debit, credit, commercial, domestic and cross-border cards, and how each is priced.
Your contract
Minimum fees, renewal terms, termination charges and price change clauses.
The market
What comparable businesses pay for the same profile.
The small print
Authorisation, refund, chargeback, PCI and statement fees that add up.

CHow we approach it

  1. 01

    Collect

    We take your recent statements and contracts from every provider you use.

  2. 02

    Separate

    We split fixed pass-through costs from the negotiable ones and rebuild your real effective rate.

  3. 03

    Benchmark

    We compare your terms with the market for a business of your size and profile.

  4. 04

    Act

    You receive a written summary and, if you choose, we renegotiate on your behalf.

DWhat it can mean for your business

  • A clear view of what you actually pay, by charge and by card type
  • Lower processing costs where the market supports it
  • Itemised statements that make future changes visible
  • Contract terms that no longer renew or rise by default
