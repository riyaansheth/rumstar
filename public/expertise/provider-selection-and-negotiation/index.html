Provider Selection and Negotiation

The right acquirer, gateway or payment provider on terms you understand, and a switch that does not interrupt trading.

  • Requirements and shortlisting
  • Structured tender
  • Contract negotiation
  • Migration planning and support

AThe problem

Providers are usually chosen once, often on a recommendation from a bank or a developer, and then left in place for years. The market moves on. Pricing, payment methods, coverage and service levels all change.

Comparing offers is harder than it looks. Headline rates hide differences in pricing models, reserves, settlement times, minimums and exit terms. And when a better offer exists, moving provider carries real operational risk.

BWhat we evaluate

Your requirements
Markets, currencies, payment methods, volumes, channels and risk profile.
Provider fit
Which acquirers, gateways and platforms genuinely suit your business.
Total cost
Offers compared like for like, not headline rate against headline rate.
Contract terms
Reserves, settlement times, liability, minimums, renewals and exit.
Integration effort
What a change would mean for your checkout and your team.
Migration risk
Stored card data, recurring payments and the go-live plan.

CHow we approach it

  1. 01

    Define

    We turn how you sell into a clear set of requirements.

  2. 02

    Tender

    We invite a shortlist of providers, including your current one, to bid.

  3. 03

    Negotiate

    We negotiate pricing and terms. You sign directly with the provider.

  4. 04

    Move

    If you switch, we plan and oversee the migration so trading continues.

DWhat it can mean for your business

  • A provider chosen for fit, not for a sales pitch
  • A fair comparison you can explain to your board
  • Better terms, whether you move or stay
  • A switch planned around your trading, not the provider's timetable
