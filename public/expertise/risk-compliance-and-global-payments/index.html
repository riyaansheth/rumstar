Risk, Compliance and Global Payments

Chargebacks, fraud, regulatory obligations, higher risk sectors and expansion into new markets.

  • Fraud and chargeback review
  • PCI DSS, SCA and EU payment rules
  • High-risk and specialist sector applications
  • New markets and local payment methods

AThe problem

Payment rules are spread across EU regulation, national law, card scheme rules and your own provider's terms. Businesses either overpay for compliance they do not need or discover a gap only when a provider steps in.

Higher risk sectors face fewer willing providers, stricter checks and rolling reserves. Businesses expanding abroad find that cards are not the default everywhere. Both need preparation before the first conversation with a provider.

BWhat we evaluate

Chargebacks
Your dispute levels against card scheme monitoring programmes.
Fraud controls
Rules that stop fraud without turning away good customers.
Obligations
PCI DSS scope, strong customer authentication and surcharging rules that apply to you.
Statement rights
Your right in the EEA to itemised pricing information.
Application readiness
For specialist sectors, the documents and controls providers will expect.
New markets
The payment methods, currencies and local acquiring your new customers use.

DWhat it can mean for your business

  • Fewer chargebacks and fewer surprises from your provider
  • Compliance effort spent where it is required
  • Stronger applications in specialist sectors
  • Payment options that match how customers pay in each market
