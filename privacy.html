Controller and contact

RUMSTAR LIMITED, HE 498715, 84 Larnakos, Pallouriotissa, 1046 Nicosia, Cyprus, is the controller of personal data used to manage its website, business enquiries and own client relationships. Contact support@rumstar.eu for privacy questions and rights requests.

Where we process personal data in client records solely on the client's instructions, the client is the controller and our agreed data-processing terms govern our processor role. This notice does not replace that client's privacy information. Roles are assessed for each engagement.

Data and sources

We may receive your name, business contact details, role, company and message when you contact us. An engagement may involve contracts, invoices, processing statements and correspondence containing information about staff, sole traders or customers. Limit and redact records before sharing them.

The enquiry form on this website does not send anything to our servers. It opens your own email application with your answers filled in, and nothing reaches us until you press send.

Your browser sends technical information to servers delivering the website. The site serves its fonts from its own server and can save an optional theme preference, and a record that you have seen the cookie notice, in your browser's local storage.

Information comes directly from you and, where relevant, authorised colleagues or business contacts. For indirectly obtained personal data, we provide the required information within the applicable GDPR timeframe unless a lawful exception applies.

Purposes and legal bases

Business enquiries and relationships. We use business-contact information to answer requests, prepare proposals and manage communications. For company representatives, our proposed basis is legitimate interests in responding to business requests and managing commercial relationships, balanced against their rights. When you personally contract, for example as a sole trader, necessary pre-contract steps you request and contract performance may instead apply.

Consultancy. The engagement identifies necessary records and our data role. As a processor, we follow the controller's documented instructions under a data-processing agreement. For any controller use of client records, we identify the specific purpose and lawful basis. A business contract alone does not authorise every use of its customers' data.

Billing and compliance. Relevant contact and transaction records are used for invoicing, accounting and applicable legal duties. Contract performance may apply for individual clients and legal obligation for statutory record-keeping.

Security and legal claims. Necessary records may be used to protect systems, investigate misuse or establish, exercise or defend claims, based on legitimate interests in security and legal rights, with appropriate limits.

Recipients and international transfers

We use service providers for business email (Google Workspace), domain name services (Cloudflare) and website hosting. Access is limited to people who need it for the relevant purpose, subject to appropriate confidentiality and contractual arrangements.

Relevant records may be shared with payment providers or other counterparties only within the engagement's agreed scope and applicable data rules, or when legally required or necessary for legal claims.

The website does not load fonts or other resources from Google; fonts are served from our own server.

Retention

We keep data only as long as needed for the stated purpose, applicable legal duties and justified claims, then delete or anonymise it under the applicable schedule.

Necessary legal holds may pause routine deletion of relevant records. Theme and cookie-notice storage remains until changed or cleared in your browser.

Rights and complaints

Subject to GDPR conditions and exceptions, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability and object to processing. You may object to legitimate-interest processing on grounds relating to your situation and to direct marketing at any time. Where consent applies, you may withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Contact support@rumstar.eu. We may ask for proportionate identity verification. We respond without undue delay, normally within one month, and explain any lawful extension within that first month. Requests are normally free, subject to GDPR rules on manifestly unfounded or excessive requests.

You may complain to the Cyprus Commissioner for Personal Data Protection at dataprotection.gov.cy or, where applicable, the authority in your country of habitual residence, work or alleged infringement. You need not complain to us first.

Information requirements, automation and updates

Enquiry information is voluntary, but missing contact details may prevent a response. An engagement may require records for the agreed review; we explain requirements before collection. Do not supply special-category data or unnecessary customer-level records unless expressly agreed and lawfully justified.

The website is directed at business users, not children. We update this notice when processing changes and provide further information or obtain consent where required. The effective date identifies the version.
