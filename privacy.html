Who we are

This policy explains how Rumstar collects and uses personal data when you visit rumstar.eu or contact us. Rumstar is the controller of that data.

Email: support@rumstar.eu. Telephone: +357 97 692104.

What we collect

When you contact us. If you email us, call us or use the enquiry form, we receive the details you choose to give: typically your name, work email, company, phone number, website and information about how your business takes payments. The enquiry form does not send anything to our servers. It opens your own email application with your answers filled in, and nothing is sent until you press send.

When you become a client. To do our work we may receive processing statements, contracts and related business documents. These are covered by our engagement terms and used only for that engagement.

When you visit the website. Like any website, the server that delivers our pages receives technical information such as your IP address and browser type in order to send the page to you. We also use privacy-friendly analytics that count visits without cookies and without identifying you. See the analytics section below.

How and why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange a conversation. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us, or steps you ask us to take before entering into a contract.
  • To provide our services to clients. Legal basis: performance of a contract.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the website is used so we can improve it. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a useful website.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations. Legal basis: legal obligation.

We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising or make automated decisions about you.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see which pages are visited and how often. It does not use cookies, does not store anything on your device and does not build a profile of you. It is provided by Cloudflare, Inc.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers who process data on our behalf:

  • Google, for business email (Google Workspace).
  • Our website hosting providers, to deliver the website.
  • Cloudflare, for domain name services and website analytics.

We share client information with payment providers only when you ask us to, for example during a tender or negotiation on your behalf. We may also disclose data where the law requires it.

International transfers

Some of our providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where that happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry correspondence for [retention period] and client records for as long as required by Cypriot tax and company law. We then delete or anonymise them.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation you have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email support@rumstar.eu. You also have the right to complain to the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection in Cyprus.

Cookies

We do not use tracking or advertising cookies. See our Cookie Policy for the small amount of information the site stores on your device.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
